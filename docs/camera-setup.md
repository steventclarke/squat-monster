# Camera Setup (V1)

- Side-on angle
- Full body and barbell visible, including full profile of the largest-diameter plate on the near side
- Plate diameter default 450 mm (selectable)
- Total weight (including bar) must be entered for force calculation
- Supports portrait and landscape; tripod preferred but not required
- 1080p/30 fps target, device defaults where unavailable
- Max 60 seconds, 100 MB; H.264/HEVC MP4/MOV
- Upload existing video (primary) or record in-app (secondary)
